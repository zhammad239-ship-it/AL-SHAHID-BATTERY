Practical Tips

Simple Battery Care Advice

Maintain optimal battery performance and avoid sudden vehicle starting failures with these general tips.

01

Check Battery Condition

Have your battery voltage tested periodically, especially if you notice subtle changes in how fast the starter engine cranks.

02

Keep Connections Clean

Ensure battery terminals are clean and free from white oxide corrosion, which can obstruct electrical conductivity.

03

Watch Starting Performance

A hesitant engine click or dim headlights before starting often indicate low battery power levels requiring immediate checking.

04

Check Before Long Trips

It is always recommended to check your battery's health status before setting out on long travel routes outside Karachi.
